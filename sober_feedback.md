# Sober Feedback: Catering Platform

**Meeting date:** October 29, 2026  
**Client:** Sober Pierre, Pearl Island2GO

## AI and customer inquiries

- The AI needs ingredient information for every menu item so it can respond to dietary questions and make appropriate suggestions.
- The system should capture Sober’s recommendations and core Pearl Island knowledge so the AI can reference his guidance when assisting customers.
- Sober receives many catering inquiries by email and wants automated email responses.

## Catering order workflow

- Orders should be organized by recency.
- Staff should be able to filter orders by date.
- The order form needs a distinct point-of-contact section because the person placing an order may differ from the pickup person or delivery recipient.

## Restaurant setup and menu management

- Onboarding should let the restaurant customize its customer-facing catering view, including its logo, color theme, and menu.
- Admins should be able to add new menu items and edit existing items, including descriptions, prices, and pictures.

## Spoonfed-informed admin features

Sober identified the following Spoonfed content as useful reference points for the catering platform:

- Dashboard
- Quotes
- Customers
- Invoices
- Costs
- Top spenders/orders, including how much a customer has spent year to date

Sober likes Spoonfed’s ratings metrics and wants a way to transfer ratings from Spoonfed into the website. He also thinks customer testimonials would be valuable on the website.

## Catering storefront visual direction

- Keep the modern feel, spacing, and clean look of the current website, which Sober liked.
- Make the customer-facing experience livelier, more colorful, and energetic, reflecting Caribbean culture.
- Use more imagery featuring Black people and hands, with an Afro-Caribbean emphasis.
