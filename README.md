# Catering

Catering helps restaurants receive complete event orders and gives their teams one place to review and run them. Customers can order with guidance or choose items themselves; staff keep control of exceptions, changes, and acceptance.

The platform is designed for different restaurants and locations. Each restaurant's brand, menu, prices, and rules are configured separately, while optional connections to websites, calendars, and other systems can be added when useful. The catering product works on its own.

Pearl Island is the first pilot we are learning with. The current app is a demo for refining the customer ordering and staff review experience; its menu and business rules are provisional, and its orders and integrations are simulated.
