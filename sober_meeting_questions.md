# Questions for Sober

Short list for the meeting. The demo is a hypothesis; every answer below changes data or rules, not the journey.

## Menu and pricing
1. Are the Spoonfed prices and dietary tags current and complete? Which items are really offered for catering?
2. Which sides and salads should go with each entree? Are any included, or is everything ordered separately?
3. Is a 6oz spoon the right unit for planning how much food a guest eats? How many servings per guest do you actually plan for, for entrees, sides and salads?
4. Are there minimum quantities per item or per order?

## Rules and policies
5. Is over 50 guests with 5 business days' notice the real rule? What about the 48 and 72 hour notice?
6. What makes an order need your personal review? What should never be accepted automatically?
7. How are changes and cancellations handled?

## Supplies
8. Which supply options can be combined, and are per-person items always priced for the full guest count? How many serving spoons, tongs and chafing sets are normal for a given headcount?

## Process
9. When do customers usually give pickup or delivery time: with the order, or later by phone?
10. What do your staff do today after an order arrives? Where does the Google Calendar step happen?
11. Which customer questions still turn into phone calls, and what would you want the software to answer for you?
12. Would a customer-led menu with optional "help me choose" suit your customers, or would they rather be guided from the start?

## Dietary
13. How should the software talk about gluten-free and vegan needs? Who confirms those?
